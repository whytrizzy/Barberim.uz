# Surface brief — Barberim Mini App (client + staff screens)

Scope: every screen of the Telegram Mini App. Mode: **Operate** (people come to get a job done).
Chosen by the user on 2026-10-29 from `docs/design/directions.html` (direction C). Dev-only document;
never copy it into source or anything shipped to the browser.

## Direction contract

**THESIS** — The app is the neighborhood barbershop (sartaroshxona) every mahalla has: tiled, clean,
the shop's name on an enamel sign. It refuses both barber-app clichés: black-and-gold "luxury" with
poles and mustaches, and the white SaaS card app with one blue.

**OWN-WORLD** — Mint tile ground with faint white grout lines; porcelain-white tiles joined by 1px
chrome-grey grout for every grouped control (masters, days, slots, lists); bottle-green ink; one
enamel red for the sign, the selected tile and the primary action. Names and headings in condensed
sign capitals (Fira Sans Extra Condensed); everything else Onest. Night version: dark green-tiled
room, same enamel red.

**STORY** — A client sees whose shop this is (the sign), picks a master, a day and a free tile, and
books with one button. A barber sees today's tiles in order, the current client marked in red, and
adds a walk-in in two taps.

**FIRST VIEWPORT** — Client: enamel sign with the shop name at top, address with landmark under it,
then masters → days → free-time tiles as joined tile groups; Telegram MainButton (enamel red) carries
"Yozilish · <time>". Staff: "BUGUN" in sign capitals, date + count + total on the right, today's
bookings as a joined tile list; the now-row's time sits on a red tile.

**FORM** — Direction C of four (A Metro, B Tashkent Modernism, C Sartaroshxona, D standard canon),
chosen by the user over my pick (A). Signature move: the enamel sign + joined tile groups with grout.
seed: none (unavailable)

**FINISH** — unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the
verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
